Who we are

BandRigs designs, builds and supports custom live mixing and in-ear monitor rigs for artists and bands in the United States. These terms apply to every quote, build, service and subscription we provide. By approving a quote or paying a deposit, you agree to them.

Quotes and system design

Before anything is ordered, we send you a written design with an equipment list, signal flow and a fixed quote. Starting prices on this site are guides. Your final price depends on the number of mixes, how much is wireless and what gear you already own. A quote is valid for 14 days, because gear prices and stock change often.

Gear you supply

If you buy your own gear, have it drop shipped to us. You are responsible for buying the right items on the equipment list and for any manufacturer warranty claims. We inspect everything when it arrives and tell you right away if something is damaged, missing or wrong. Extra time caused by wrong or faulty parts is billed at $125/hr.

Changes to a build

If you change the design after gear is ordered, we send an updated quote. Extra gear and time are added to your balance. Items already opened or configured may not be returnable.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel before we order gear, we refund your deposit in full. After gear is ordered, we refund your deposit minus any supplier restocking fees, return shipping and build time already completed. Custom parts, such as a backlit name panel, are not refundable once made. The monthly support plan can be cancelled at any time and stops at the end of the paid month. The annual plan is not refundable, and your support continues to the end of the year you paid for.

Shipping and delivery

Finished rigs ship insured to the address you give us. Check the rig when it arrives and tell us within 3 days about any shipping damage, so we can file a claim with the carrier. Delivery dates are estimates, because they depend on gear availability and carriers.

Warranty

All gear carries the manufacturer’s own warranty, and we help you use it. We stand behind our workmanship, including wiring, connectors, labeling and configuration, for 90 days after delivery. If something we built fails in that time, we fix it at no charge for labor. This does not cover damage from drops, liquids, misuse, unapproved changes or normal wear.

Service & support plan

The plan covers remote help with your rig, firmware updates and mix or scene changes, during normal business hours. It does not cover replacement gear, in-person visits or shipping, which we quote separately.
