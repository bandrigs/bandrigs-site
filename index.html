Why BandRigs exists

BandRigs builds complete sound and in-ear systems for working bands that want a consistent show without becoming audio engineers.

Our team has over 40 years of performing experience. We know firsthand what artists go through: rushed load-ins, monitor mixes you can’t hear, and a different room every night.

For solo artists, duos, tribute and cover bands, worship teams and touring acts anywhere in the US.

You got into music to play, not to spend an hour running cables and chasing feedback. Most bands sound great in rehearsal, then fight every room they walk into: a new stage, a new house engineer, monitor wedges that squeal, and a mix nobody on stage can really hear. Our custom in-ear monitor (IEM) and live mixing rigs let you spend more time in the music and less time setting up gear.

Everything lives in one rolling rack, wired, labeled and tested before it ships. Your monitor mixes are saved on a digital mixer like the Behringer WING, the players who move get wireless in-ear monitors, and playback with click and cues runs from the same rack. Roll it in, plug in power and a couple of cables, and you are line checked and ready to play.

Why this gear? We build around Behringer, Midas and Shure because it is solid on the road, sounds great and gives working bands pro-level features at a price that fits a real budget. It is the gear we would put on our own stage, and it is easy to service and replace anywhere in the country.

In-ear monitor rig FAQ

Do I need to know what gear I want?

No. Tell us who is on stage, what you play, the rooms you usually work and what frustrates you about your current setup. Picking the mixer, stage box, in-ear systems and rack is our job. You get a written design with an equipment list and signal flow before anything is ordered, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

What does the price include?

Every starting price includes the gear and the build: racking, wiring, labeling, configuring your mixes and testing the whole system on our bench. Shipping the finished rig to you is extra. Your final price depends on how many personal mixes you need, how many players go wireless and what gear you already own.

Can I keep the equipment I already own?

Yes. We look at your mixer, stage boxes, in-ear systems and cables first and only replace what does not fit the plan. Keeping gear that still works lowers your price, and we will tell you honestly when something is worth replacing.

Can I buy the gear myself?

Yes. We send you the equipment list, you buy it from any dealer and have it drop shipped to us. We build, configure and test the rig at our service rate of $125 an hour, plus shipping the finished rig back to you.

Wired or wireless in-ears?

Either, or a mix. Drummers and keyboard players stay in one spot, so wired in-ears are simple and cost less. Singers, guitarists and anyone who moves around usually go wireless with a system like the Shure PSM 300. We plan the mix of wired and wireless around how your band actually performs.

What is a personal mix?

It is what one performer hears in their in-ears. The drummer might want lots of bass and click, the singer mostly vocals and keys. Each person who wants their own balance needs their own mix, and the number of mixes is one of the biggest things that sets the size of your rig. Each player adjusts their own mix from their own iPad or phone over the rig’s Wi-Fi, without waiting on a sound engineer, and the mixes are saved in the mixer so they sound the same every night.

How much time will this save at load-in?

A lot. The rack arrives prewired and labeled, so instead of running cables and stands for every wedge and channel you connect power, the stage box and a few lines. Because your monitor mixes are already dialed in, sound check becomes a quick line check and a clean feed to the house engineer.

Can you run our backing tracks and click?

Yes. Playback with click and cues can run from the same rack, from a Mac mini or a playback interface like the iConnectivity PlayAUDIO1U. The PlayAUDIO1U adds redundancy: two Mac minis run the same show in sync, and if the main one stops, it switches to the backup automatically so the tracks, click and cues keep playing and the audience never knows. We can also build your playback tracks in Ableton, with your stems, click and cues set up as a show-ready session. Call for pricing on track building.

What if something goes wrong after I get it?

We walk you through the rig and hand over a setup guide when it is done. For ongoing help, our service and support plan adds to any rig. It is $75 a month, or $50 a month when you pay for a year up front, and covers remote support, firmware updates and mix changes when you need them.

Do you work with bands outside your area?

Yes. We design and build rigs for artists and bands anywhere in the US. Planning happens on calls, and the finished rig ships to you ready to plug in.
