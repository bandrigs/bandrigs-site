Bands of 3 to 6

Every player hears their own mix.

Built for bands of 3 to 6. Four players get their own wireless in-ear mix (add two more for a six-piece), the whole band plugs into one stage box, and you run it all from a tablet. One rolling rack, the same setup every night.

Starting at $9,000
Plan this rig Call 858-232-3544

What’s included

  • Mix plan: who hears what, and how it connects
  • Your mixer, stage box and wireless in-ears racked together
  • Four wireless in-ear systems, one mix per player
  • Wi-Fi router so each player can adjust their mix from a phone
  • Wired, labeled and tested, with saved starting mixes
  • Setup guide and a walkthrough with your band

A good fit if…

  • Your band has 3 to 6 players
  • Your band uses wedges and nobody can hear
  • Each player wants a different mix
  • You want load-in to be the same every night
  • You need an A/B splitter snake option for FOH
