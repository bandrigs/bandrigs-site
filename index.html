Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know what gear I want?

No. Tell us who plays and what bugs you about your current setup. Picking the gear is our job.

Can I keep the equipment I already own?

Yes. We look at your mixer, stage boxes and in-ear gear first and only replace what doesn’t fit the plan.

Wired or wireless in-ears?

Either, or a mix. Drummers and keys players are often fine wired. Singers and anyone who moves around usually go wireless.

What is a personal mix?

It’s what one performer hears in their in-ears. Each person who wants their own balance of vocals and instruments needs their own mix.
