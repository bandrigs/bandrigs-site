Two ways to pay

  • Bank transfer (ACH) Recommended

    Pay straight from your checking account. It’s the simplest way to pay a large deposit, with no card limits. Clears in about 3 to 5 business days.

  • Credit or debit card

    Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Clears right away.

Online payment opens soon. Until then, call or email and we’ll send you a secure payment link.

Payments are processed by Helcim. We never see or store your full card or bank account number.

How payments work

Rig builds are paid in two parts.

  1. 01

    You approve the quote

    75% deposit. We order your gear as soon as it clears.

  2. 02

    We build and test

    Every rig is bench tested. We send you photos or video of the finished rig.

  3. 03

    Before it ships

    25% balance, plus shipping and any sales tax. Your rig ships once it’s paid in full.

Quotes are good for 14 days. Builds with gear you buy yourself, playback tracks and the support plan have their own schedules. See our Terms of Service.
