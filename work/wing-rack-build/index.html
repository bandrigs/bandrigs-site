How it comes together

The WING Rack runs the front-of-house mix and every performer’s in-ear mix. The S16 sits on stage and carries the band’s inputs over a single network cable. The DN4816-O adds analog outputs for wired in-ear packs, and the PSM 300 transmitters send wireless mixes to the players who move around.

Everything was racked, labeled and tested together before delivery, along with a setup guide.
